# HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze employee attrition, demographics, job roles, salary ranges, education fields, and employee tenure.

The dashboard transforms HR data into meaningful visual insights that can help organizations understand employee attrition patterns and identify areas that may require attention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall employee attrition.
- Understand attrition patterns across different age groups.
- Analyze attrition by education field.
- Identify job roles with higher employee attrition.
- Examine salary slabs associated with attrition.
- Compare attrition between male and female employees.
- Analyze employee tenure and average salary by job role.
- Present HR metrics through an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes the following major HR metrics:

- **Total Employees:** 1,480
- **Attrition Count:** 238
- **Attrition Rate:** ~16%
- **Average Age:** 36.92 years
- **Average Tenure:** 7.01 years
- **Average Salary:** 6,504.99

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Attrition by Education Field

The dashboard compares employee attrition across different educational backgrounds.

Key categories include:

- Life Sciences
- Medical
- Marketing
- Technical Degree
- Other
- Human Resources

Life Sciences and Medical backgrounds account for a significant portion of the observed attrition.

---

### 2. Attrition by Age Group

Employee attrition is analyzed across different age groups:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

The **26–35 age group** records the highest attrition count in the dashboard.

---

### 3. Gender and Attrition Analysis

A donut chart is used to analyze employee attrition based on gender.

This helps identify differences in attrition patterns between male and female employees.

---

### 4. Job Role Analysis

The dashboard provides a detailed comparison of:

- Job Role
- Attrition Count
- Average Years
- Average Salary

This analysis helps identify job roles experiencing higher employee turnover and understand differences in salary and tenure.

---

### 5. Attrition by Salary Slab

Employee attrition is categorized into salary ranges:

- Up to 5K
- 5K–10K
- 10K–15K
- 15K+

The dashboard indicates that the **lowest salary slab has the highest attrition count**.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- The organization has **1,480 employees**.
- **238 employees** have left the organization.
- The overall attrition rate is approximately **16%**.
- Employees in the **26–35 age group** show the highest attrition.
- The **Life Sciences** education field has the highest attrition count among the displayed education categories.
- Employees in the **lowest salary slab** account for the highest number of attrition cases.
- Attrition varies considerably across different job roles.
- The dashboard can help HR teams identify employee groups that may require better retention strategies.

> **Note:** These observations are based on the dataset used for this dashboard and should be interpreted within the context of the available data.
